Step 1 of 7 - Protein Level

Choose your protein level

Pick your pure protein base. Your add-ons will boost it even further.

More protein = more calories. That's just how it works. We engineer every bar for the best taste with the lowest possible calories at each protein level.

Texture tip: the more protein you pack in, the drier and more powdery the bar will feel. Lower protein levels come out softer and fudgier.
